Good to know

Can I pay for friends or family?

Yes. Choose the number of tickets, send the total in one M-Pesa payment and add their names. One QR code admits your whole group, so come in together.

How long until I get my ticket?

As soon as the treasurer matches your M-Pesa code, usually within a day. Use "Check your ticket status" above any time.

I didn't get the email

Check your spam or promotions folder first. Still nothing? Reach us on WhatsApp and we'll resend it.

What do I show at the entrance?

The QR code in your ticket email, on your phone or printed (a PDF copy is attached). Ushers scan it and you're in.

When is the RSVP deadline?
